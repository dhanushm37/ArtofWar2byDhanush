Game Guide & CDP Data

How to Play: Deploy your 5 types of stickman troops in your 6x6 blue region. Combine identical troops and levels to upgrade them up to Level 10. Click 'Okay' to view the battlefield, then 'START' to battle the enemy red tower!

Troop Types & Mechanics:

  • Swordsmen: Close-range fighters with sword & helmet.
  • Archer: Ranged unit with arrows that fade in flight and curve over distance.
  • Spearmen Guard: Heavy defense unit with Spartan helmet, shield, and waving scarf.
  • Catapult: Siege unit launching massive burning rocks with torch ignition. Excess damage splashes to nearby enemies!
  • Magician: Unique mystic unit summoning a cloud with a wand to strike enemies with lightning. Excess damage splashes!
